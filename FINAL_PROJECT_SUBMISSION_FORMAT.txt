4. PROJECT ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flooding caused by intense localized precipitation represents one of the most 
destructive natural disasters worldwide. Traditional riverbank monitoring 
infrastructures are reactive—triggering warnings only after retention banks have 
physically breached. 

This project presents an Automated, End-to-End Predictive Flood Early Warning 
System (FEWS). The architecture integrates continuous precipitation and river 
gauge telemetry streamed over HiveMQ Cloud (MQTT with TLS 8883), a 2-layer Long 
Short-Term Memory (LSTM) deep learning forecasting engine in PyTorch (achieving 
R² = 99.73%), and interactive dual operational dashboards implemented in 
Node-RED and Streamlit with an interactive Geographical GIS flood map. 

By modeling non-linear catchment runoff delay dynamics, the system predicts water 
level crests 15–60 minutes in advance and automatically triggers multi-tiered early 
warning protocols (Safe, Watch, Warning, Critical) to empower proactive disaster 
management.

5. KEY MATHEMATICAL & AI MODEL RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Catchment Inflow Eq.   : h(t) = h_base + (h(t-1) - h_base)*0.92 + [0.35*R(t) + 0.45*R(t-1) + 0.20*R(t-2)]*0.048
• Neural Architecture    : 2-Layer LSTM (Hidden Dim=64, Dropout=0.2) + Dense ReLU (32) + Linear (1)
• Coefficient of Det.    : R² Score = 99.73%
• Root Mean Square Error : RMSE = 0.0476 meters (~4.7 cm)
• Mean Absolute Error    : MAE = 0.0217 meters (~2.1 cm)
• Inference Latency      : < 12 milliseconds per cycle

6. OPERATIONAL SAFETY THRESHOLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Level < 1.50 m         : SAFE (Green)    - Routine continuous monitoring
• Level 1.50 m - 2.00 m  : WATCH (Yellow)  - Moderate runoff; alert drainage crews
• Level 2.00 m - 2.50 m  : WARNING (Orange)- High risk; mobilize sandbags & floodgates
• Level > 2.50 m         : CRITICAL (Red)  - Imminent breach; sound community sirens
